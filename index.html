Who We Are

Every Journey Matters.

As a sports parent watching his daughter compete in gymnastics and track, founder Carlos Chalmers picked up a camera to preserve moments he knew would pass too quickly. Those videos soon helped capture the attention of college recruiters, and a parent's desire to document the journey grew into a passion for helping others share theirs.

Today, Chalmers Media connects sports, recruiting, and lifestyle through photography and videography, capturing both the performance and the person behind it.
