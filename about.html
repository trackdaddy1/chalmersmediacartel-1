About

Our Story

What started with a father capturing his daughter's journey continues with a purpose: helping others celebrate who they are.

As a sports parent watching his daughter compete in gymnastics and track, founder Carlos Chalmers picked up a camera to preserve moments he knew would pass too quickly. Each routine, race, and milestone told a story of dedication, growth, and passion.

Those videos soon served a purpose beyond family memories: they helped capture the attention of college recruiters. Seeing how footage could showcase his daughter’s abilities and bring visibility to her hard work sparked a larger vision. A parent’s desire to document the journey grew into a passion for helping others share theirs.

Today, Chalmers Media connects sports, recruiting, and lifestyle through photography and videography. From highlights that help athletes introduce themselves to coaches to portraits and everyday moments that express their personality, we capture both the performance and the person behind it.

Our work is rooted in a parent’s understanding that every journey matters—the preparation, the setbacks, the breakthroughs, and the people who make it possible. We create imagery that helps talent get seen, gives personal stories a voice, and preserves memories that grow more meaningful with time.

What started with a father capturing his daughter’s journey continues with a purpose: helping others celebrate who they are and show the world what they can become.

Our Mission

Our mission at Chalmers Media is to bring stories into focus that deserve to be seen. Through powerful imagery and authentic storytelling, we honor the work behind every achievement, amplify emerging talent, and help athletes, creators, and communities build lasting legacies. We capture moments so that the dedication, identity, and journey behind them are remembered.
